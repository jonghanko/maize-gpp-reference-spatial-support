Figure 1. Controlled comparisons and saved-model cross evaluation. (a) The principal experiment has 642 daily cases from four site-seasons, three towers and two regions; the separate Mead hindcast trains on 480 later-year cases and tests 437 earlier-year dates at seen sites. Cases, meteorology, model recipes and validation roles are fixed within each comparison. Every prediction is scored against the two dependent supplied nighttime GPP paths. (b) Original B/C models are tested on both B and C inputs, retaining 100 principal and 20 hindcast objects without refitting. Rows denote training and columns test representation. Diagonal cells reproduce original assignments; off-diagonal cells give conditional training/test contrasts and interaction. These post-review exposed-domain sensitivities are not physical causal effects or independent confirmation.

Figure 2. Published monthly retained-core support on the frozen MODIS grid. Fixed July examples show Ne1 and Ne3 in 2013 and Ro1 in 2015. Blue fills/labels indicate conditional core mass assigned to whole cells; B uses uniform weights over the same positive-mass cells. Orange/black geometry shows the original DAY retained-core rasters (Chu et al., 2020), normalized from the retained 80% core. Purple outlines indicate point-containing cells; circles/crosses show registered/published coordinates. Ne1's inset resolves their 5.57 m WGS84 separation across a cell edge, without relocation or period-valid mast adjudication. Native Sinusoidal cells are 463.313 m wide (nominal 500 m); arrows show geographic north. The display establishes neither field boundaries, crop purity nor complete daily footprints.

Figure 3. Conditional representation contrasts under equal-site-season RMSE. Rows distinguish the principal 642-case experiment and separate 437-date hindcast; columns show Ridge and RF. Symbols distinguish dependent own-fit/own-score VUT/CUT paths. Training contrasts hold test inputs fixed; test-input contrasts hold the fitted model fixed. First indices denote training, second test inputs. Interaction is R_CC−R_CB−R_BC+R_BB and pipeline difference R_CC−R_BB. Negative conditional/pipeline differences favor C; an interaction sign is not itself a gain. All original cases/models remain fixed. Ridge and RF are principal comparators, with Ridge secondary in the hindcast. No population uncertainty bars are implied. RMSE nonlinearity and aggregation contribute to interaction, preventing unique additive causal attribution.

Figure 4. Three original LOYO input contrasts on 642 cases. Contrasts are C minus B, B minus shared-scene point A, and C minus shared-scene point A. The upper row averages four site-season RMSEs; the lower row averages two region-specific RMSEs after pooling daily residuals within each region. Both pooling and weighting change. Columns retain the primary model families and symbols the two dependent own-fit/own-score paths. Negative values favor the first-named representation. B minus A is a post-review contrast of frozen outputs. Individual site-seasons appear in Figure S6. The two-region descriptive summaries have no population uncertainty bars.
