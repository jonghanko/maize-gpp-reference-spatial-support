Graphical Abstract. A matched-case maize gross primary production (GPP) benchmark compares uniform-cell MODIS inputs (B) with normalized monthly retained-core weights (C). B and C share cells, accepted scenes, meteorology, observations and validation roles. Stored B- and C-trained models are tested on both representations without refitting. In the matrix, the first letter denotes training and the second test input; fitting/scoring references and aggregation are held fixed within each four-cell comparison. The principal random-forest (RF) callout shows 2.20-2.51% lower equal-site-season root mean square error (RMSE) for CC versus BB across the dependent own-fit/own-score VUT/CUT reference paths. However, C versus B training lowers RMSE at fixed B test input and raises it at fixed C test input. The 642-case principal cohort and separate 437-case Mead hindcast are not pooled; the 120 saved models comprise 100 principal and 20 hindcast objects. These are conditional historical comparisons, not physical daily footprint correction or independent forecast/transfer validation.
