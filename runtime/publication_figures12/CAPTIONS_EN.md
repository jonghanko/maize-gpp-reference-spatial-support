# Publication figure captions

Article: Training–Test Representation and Reference-Path Sensitivity in a Satellite-Based Maize Gross Primary Production Benchmark

Author-reviewed manuscript version: v0.13, prepared 27 October 2026.

Fig. 1. Controlled comparisons and saved-model cross-evaluation. (a) Principal LOYO equally averages four maize site-season RMSEs from 642 common cases, three towers, and two regions. The separate Mead hindcast trains on 480 later-year training cases (2012/2013) and tests 437 earlier-year test cases (2010/2011) at seen sites. A is the existing point input, A_shared the shared-scene point, B uniform cells and C conditional-core weighting; the hindcast compares B/C only. Each comparison fixes cases, seven AgERA5 predictors, supported folds, recipes, all five model families, and original priorities. B/C share cells and accepted scenes. Arms fit VUT or CUT separately; every prediction is scored against both dependent nighttime GPP paths sharing observations. Equal-season and equal-region pooled summaries remain distinct. (b) Rows denote training, columns test inputs; f_B/f_C are original fitted models, and R_BB/R_BC/R_CB/R_CC their scores. Blue diagonal cells retain original assignments; orange off-diagonal cells are cross-predictions from unchanged objects. The 100 principal and 20 hindcast B/C objects yield 240 prediction passes and zero new fits, with fitting/scoring paths separate. Conditional contrasts and interaction diagnose retrospective, post-review exposed-domain sensitivities, without physical causal attribution or independent confirmation.

Fig. 2. Published monthly retained-core support on the frozen MODIS grid. Fixed July illustrations show (a) US-Ne1, 2013; (b) US-Ne3, 2013; and (c) US-Ro1, 2015, without representing all months. Blue fills and three-decimal labels give C's conditional retained-core mass in whole cells. B uses the same positive-mass cells: B=0.500/0.500 and C=0.640/0.360 in (a); B=0.500/0.500 and C=0.983/0.017 in (b); B=C=1.000 in (c), with one selected cell. Orange/black geometry retains original DAY rasters [16], normalized from the retained 80% core. Purple outlines identify A's containing cell; circles/crosses show registered input/published footprint coordinates. The unlabelled inset in (a) resolves Ne1's grid edge and 5.57 m WGS84 geodesic separation without relocation or period-valid mast adjudication. Native Sinusoidal cells are 463.313 m wide (nominal 500 m); axes give offsets from the registered coordinate and arrows geographic north. Actual field boundaries are unavailable; this display establishes neither field boundaries, crop purity nor complete daily footprints.

Fig. 3. Conditional representation contrasts under equal-site-season RMSE: (a) principal Ridge, (b) principal RF, (c) separate Mead-hindcast Ridge (secondary), and (d) Mead-hindcast RF. Principal panels retain 642 cases, four site-seasons and two regions; hindcast panels retain 437 cases, three site-seasons and one existing region. Symbols distinguish dependent own-fit/own-score VUT/CUT paths. “Train C−B | B/C” compares training representations at fixed B/C test input; “Test C−B | B/C” compares test inputs within B-/C-trained models. First indices denote training, second test inputs. Interaction is R_CC−R_CB−R_BC+R_BB; pipeline difference is R_CC−R_BB. Negative conditional/pipeline differences favor C; interaction sign is not itself a gain. All original cases/models remain fixed, without refitting. Ridge and RF remain principal comparators; no population uncertainty bars are implied. RMSE nonlinearity and aggregation contribute to interaction, preventing unique additive causal attribution.

Fig. 4. Three original LOYO input contrasts on 642 cases: C−B, B−A_shared and C−A_shared, where A_shared is the shared-scene point. Panels show (a) Ridge and (b) RF under the mean of four site-season RMSEs, and (c) Ridge and (d) RF under the mean of two region-pooled RMSEs. The latter first pools daily residuals within region; both pooling and weighting change. Colors retain the two dependent own-fit/own-score paths. Blue upper and orange lower numerical rows below each axis report four-decimal VUT and CUT values, respectively. Negative values favor the first-named representation. B−A_shared is a post-review descriptive contrast of frozen outputs; C−A_shared remains conditional on point registration. Individual site-seasons appear in Fig. S6. These two-region descriptive summaries have no population uncertainty bars.

Fig. S1. Signed relative C−B RMSE sensitivity to site-season omissions from fixed LOYO out-of-fold error summaries. The full column contains four site-seasons; each specified omission leaves three, in unchanged row/column order. Blue indicates lower C error and orange higher C error; all 20 cells share one zero-centered color scale. Models and predictions remain unchanged: these evaluation-summary omissions involve no retraining, new validation or uncertainty intervals. Omitting Ro1 leaves only Mead in evaluation while retaining Ro1 labels in the original training folds. This is a post-review descriptive sensitivity.

Fig. S2. Separately frozen earlier-year Mead hindcast: train 2012/2013 (480 training cases), test 2010/2011 (437 cases), within one existing region. Panels (a) RF (primary) and (b) Ridge (secondary) show equal-test-site-season RMSE for B/C. Panel (c) retains every corresponding site-season C−B percentage in the same 3×4 order: Ne1-2010 (128 cases), Ne1-2011 (148) and Ne3-2011 (161). Negative percentages indicate lower C RMSE. VUT/CUT are paired own-fit/own-score paths from dependent processed GPP references; inputs use fixed monthly retained 80% DAY cores. Three correlated site-seasons and two dependent references supply neither independent regional replications nor population uncertainty intervals. This retrospective comparison is neither a regional-transfer nor forecast test. All five fixed families remain in Tables S17–S19.
